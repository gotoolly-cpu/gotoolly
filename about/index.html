Why GoToolly Exists

Most online tools require uploading files to servers, creating accounts, or accepting privacy trade-offs for basic tasks.

Simple tasks like compressing an image or counting words in a document should not require uploading sensitive files to a server, creating an account, or reading a long privacy policy. GoToolly was created to remove that friction.

By processing files locally in your browser with JavaScript, we deliver the functionality you need without file uploads, account registration, or data collection. You can work with sensitive documents privately, start using a tool immediately, and keep full control of your data from start to finish.

Each tool is designed to solve one task well, with a clean interface and predictable results. This approach is a deliberate trade-off: some complex features are not included because they genuinely require server-side compute. When that is the case we link to alternatives and explain the difference, rather than silently uploading your data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to the questions we hear most often.

Is GoToolly free?

Yes. All current tools are free to use. No account registration, email verification, or payment information is required.

Are my files safe?

Yes. All file operations happen locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, stored, or transmitted.

Do you collect my data?

We do not collect the files or text you process. We may collect limited, standard website information such as pages visited and browser type to operate the service.

How is GoToolly funded?

We display non-intrusive advertising and may include affiliate links for relevant products. Revenue supports hosting costs and ongoing development.

What if a tool does not work for me?

Tools depend on modern browser capabilities. Try updating your browser, or contact us with specific details so we can help.

Can I suggest a new tool?

Yes. Tell us the specific problem the tool would solve. We focus on tools that work in browsers without uploading files.
