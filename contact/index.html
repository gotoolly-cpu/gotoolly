Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions before you reach out.

What kind of support do you provide?

We help with technical issues, answer questions about tool functionality, and consider suggestions. We do not provide custom development or external system integration.

How should I suggest new tools?

Describe the specific problem the tool would solve and who would use it. We focus on browser-based tools that serve general rather than specialized needs.

Do you offer custom development services?

No. We maintain our existing tools and occasionally add new ones that align with our privacy-focused approach.

How do I report security issues?

Email security@gotoolly.com with reproduction steps and any supporting evidence. We address valid reports promptly.

How soon will I get a reply?

We typically respond within 1-3 business days. Bug reports and security issues receive priority attention.

Will my information be shared?

No. Messages are handled confidentially and used only to respond to your inquiry. They are never shared with third parties.
