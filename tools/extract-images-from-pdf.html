Frequently Asked Questions

What types of images can be extracted?

Only embedded raster images (PNG, JPEG) are extracted. Text, vector graphics, and annotations are ignored.

Does my file leave my computer?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.

Is extracting images free?

Yes, extracting images is completely free with no limits.

What image format are extracted images?

Images are exported as PNG format to preserve quality.
