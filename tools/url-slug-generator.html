FAQ

Common questions about the URL Slug Generator.

What is a URL slug?

A URL slug is the part of a web address that identifies a page in a readable way, such as how-to-brew-coffee. Good slugs are lowercase, short and keyword-focused.

Which separator should I use?

Google recommends hyphens (-) as word separators. Underscores are treated as joining words, so hyphens are almost always the better choice.

Why remove stopwords?

Stopwords like the, a and of rarely add meaning and make slugs longer. Removing them keeps URLs short and scannable while staying readable.

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
