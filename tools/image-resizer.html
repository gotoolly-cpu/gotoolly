Frequently Asked Questions

How do I resize without losing quality?

Keep the aspect ratio locked and avoid upscaling beyond 200%. Use PNG or WebP output for lossless results.

Can I resize multiple images?

This tool resizes one image at a time. For batch resizing, use the Image Compressor.

What formats are supported?

JPG, PNG, and WebP input. Output can be saved as JPG, PNG, or WebP.
