Frequently Asked Questions

What image formats can I use?

You can use JPG, PNG, and WebP images. The tool works entirely in your browser.

Can I customize the text color and size?

Yes. You can set custom font size, text color, and stroke color using the controls.

Does the meme text use the Impact font?

Yes. The tool uses the Impact font by default with a black outline, which is the classic meme style.

Is my image uploaded to any server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device.
