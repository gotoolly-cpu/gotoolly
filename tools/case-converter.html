Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text stored on a server?

No. All conversion happens locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

What is camelCase?

camelCase joins words without spaces, capitalizing the first letter of each word except the first (e.g. myVariableName).

What is Title Case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of each word (e.g. Hello World).

Is there a character limit?

There is no enforced limit. You can convert very large texts without issues.
