FAQ

Common questions about robots.txt validation.

How does the robots.txt validator work?

Paste your robots.txt content and the tool parses the rules, checks for syntax errors, and shows which paths are allowed or blocked for different bots.

What errors does it detect?

It detects invalid directives, missing user-agent blocks, syntax errors, unknown directives, and common configuration mistakes like paths without a leading slash.

Are wildcards allowed in robots.txt?

Yes. Google fully supports the "*" and "$" wildcards in paths, but Bing supports them only partially and many smaller crawlers ignore them. The tool flags them as compatibility warnings, not errors.

Is my robots.txt data stored?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.
