Frequently Asked Questions

How does it work?

Paste your text, configure options, and click Remove Duplicates. Only the first occurrence of each line is kept.

Can I sort alphabetically?

Yes. Enable the Sort alphabetically option before processing.

Is case sensitivity supported?

Yes. By default it is case-insensitive. Enable Case-sensitive to treat different cases as unique.

Is my text uploaded?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

What is the maximum input size?

500 KB of text per operation.

Is GoToolly free?

Yes. No account, no limits, no payment.
