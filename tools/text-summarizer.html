Frequently Asked Questions

How does the summarizer work?

It uses extractive summarization. Sentences are scored by word frequency and position, then the highest-scoring ones are selected to form the summary.

Can I control the summary length?

Yes. Use the length slider to set the summary to 25%, 50%, or 75% of the original text.

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
