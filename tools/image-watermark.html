Frequently Asked Questions

What formats are supported?

You can add watermarks to JPG, PNG, and WebP images up to 25 MB. The tool processes everything in your browser.

Can I adjust the opacity?

Yes. You can set the opacity from 5% to 80% using the slider. Lower opacity creates a subtle watermark.

Can I rotate the text?

Yes. You can rotate the watermark from -90 to +90 degrees. A -45 degree angle is commonly used for diagonal watermarks.

Is my image uploaded?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device.
