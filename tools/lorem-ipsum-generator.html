FAQ

Common questions about the Lorem Ipsum generator.

What is Lorem Ipsum?

Lorem Ipsum is scrambled, Latin-derived placeholder text used to preview layouts before the real content is written.

Can I choose paragraphs, sentences or words?

Yes. Pick the text type, set a quantity from 1 to 100, and generate exactly the content you need.

What does “Start with Lorem ipsum” do?

When enabled, the first paragraph begins with the classic phrase “Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit.”

Is my generated text sent to a server?

No. All text is produced locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded, tracked, or stored.

Can I copy the result?

Yes. Click Copy Text and paste the placeholder straight into any editor, design tool or prototype.

Why is placeholder text useful?

It lets designers and developers fill layouts with realistic-length content to check spacing, typography and flow before final copy exists.
