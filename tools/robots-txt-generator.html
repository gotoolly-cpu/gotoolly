FAQ

Common questions about robots.txt files.

What is a robots.txt file?

A robots.txt file tells search engine crawlers which pages or directories they can or cannot access on your website.

How do I use the generated robots.txt?

Copy the generated text and place it in the root directory of your website (e.g., https://example.com/robots.txt).

Should every group have rules?

Yes. Each user-agent group should include at least one Allow or Disallow rule, otherwise it has no effect.
