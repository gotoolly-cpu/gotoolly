FAQ

Common questions about the SERP Preview tool.

What does the SERP Preview tool show?

It shows side-by-side desktop and mobile Google search result previews with your title, URL, description, and breadcrumb path.

How accurate is the preview?

The preview closely approximates Google's search result display, including character limits and truncation behavior.

Why should titles stay under 60 characters?

Google truncates titles around 60 characters on desktop. Longer titles get cut off, which reduces their impact in the results.
