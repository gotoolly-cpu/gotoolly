Frequently Asked Questions

Is adding text to PDF free?

Yes, adding text to PDFs is completely free with no limits.

Can I choose where the text appears?

Yes. Place text anywhere on the page by clicking, then drag to reposition. Use the 9-position grid for quick placement or fine-tune with X and Y offsets.

Does my file leave my computer?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your PDF never leaves your device.

What fonts are supported?

The tool uses Helvetica, a standard PDF font that is supported by all PDF readers.
