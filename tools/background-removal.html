Frequently Asked Questions

How does background removal work?

The tool uses AI-powered edge detection to identify the subject of your image and separate it from the background. The processed result has a transparent background that can be placed on any color or design.

What image formats are supported?

The tool supports JPG, PNG, WebP, and GIF input images up to 20MB. The result can be downloaded as PNG (with transparency), WebP, or JPG.

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your image never leaves your device, ensuring complete privacy.

Can I refine the result?

Yes. The tool includes brush tools (Add/Remove) for manual mask refinement, edge cleanup, and an enhance option for better results.
