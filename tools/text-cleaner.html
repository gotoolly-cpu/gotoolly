Frequently Asked Questions

Is my text stored on a server?

No. All cleaning happens locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

What cleaning options are available?

You can remove extra spaces, empty lines, special characters, and trim line whitespace.

Can I undo the cleaning?

You can modify the original text in the input area and clean again with different options.

Is there a text size limit?

There is no enforced limit. You can clean very large texts without issues.
