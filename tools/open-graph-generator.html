FAQ

Common questions about Open Graph meta tags.

Why do I need Open Graph tags?

Open Graph tags control how your page appears when shared on Facebook, LinkedIn, Slack and other platforms. They define the title, description and image in the share preview.

Which properties are required?

The core properties are og:title, og:type, og:url and og:image. This tool flags missing or invalid core properties in its analysis checklist.

Can I use Open Graph and Twitter Card together?

Yes. Twitter falls back to Open Graph if Twitter Card tags are absent, but for full control add both. Use the Twitter Card Generator for the matching tags.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.
