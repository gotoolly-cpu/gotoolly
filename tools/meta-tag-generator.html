Frequently Asked Questions

What meta tags does this generator create?

It generates title, description, keywords, author, robots, viewport, canonical, Open Graph, Twitter Card, theme-color, favicon and PWA meta tags for your webpage.

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

How is the SEO score calculated?

The score checks 10 key areas: title, description, canonical URL, robots, Open Graph, Twitter Cards, favicons and technical tags such as viewport, theme color and charset. Each area is worth 10 points.
