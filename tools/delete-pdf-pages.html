Frequently Asked Questions

Is deleting PDF pages free?

Yes, deleting pages from PDF is completely free.

Can I undo page deletion?

Yes, pages are marked for deletion but not removed until you click apply. You can unmark any page before applying.

Does my file leave my computer?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser.

Can I delete pages by range?

Yes, you can enter page ranges (e.g., 1-3, 5) to mark multiple pages for deletion at once.
