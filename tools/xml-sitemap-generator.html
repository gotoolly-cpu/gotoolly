FAQ

Common questions about the XML Sitemap Generator.

What is an XML sitemap?

An XML sitemap is a file that lists all the pages on your website, helping search engines like Google discover and crawl your content efficiently.

How do I use the generated sitemap?

Download the XML file and upload it to your website's root directory. Then submit the URL to Google Search Console.

What is a good priority value?

Priorities range from 0.0 to 1.0 and are relative hints, not absolute rank signals. Use 1.0 only for your most important pages.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. All processing happens in your browser. Your URLs never leave your device.
