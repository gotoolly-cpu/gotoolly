Frequently Asked Questions

How many keywords can I generate?

You can generate up to 50 keyword ideas per search. There is no daily limit.

Is the keyword data stored?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your keywords never leave your device.

Can I export the keywords?

Yes. You can copy individual keywords or copy the full list as comma-separated text.

Are the keywords searchable?

The tool generates keyword ideas based on your input, but does not provide search volume data.
