Frequently Asked Questions

Does the word counter store my text?

No. All counting happens locally in your browser. Your text is never sent to any server.

What counts as a word?

Any sequence of characters separated by spaces counts as a word, including numbers and URLs.

How is reading time calculated?

Based on an average reading speed of 200 words per minute for English text.

Is there a character limit?

There is no enforced limit. You can paste very large texts and the counter will handle them.
